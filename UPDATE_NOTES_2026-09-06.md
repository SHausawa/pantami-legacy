# Pantami Legacy Digital Archive - Profile Expansion Update

This update expands the original project explorer into a broader digital legacy archive while keeping the evidence-first methodology.

## New sections

- Bilingual biography based on the supplied legacy compilation
- Education and academic career
- NITDA leadership record
- Ministerial service and digital-economy reforms
- Global service, professional standing and international engagement
- Achievement figures with direct links to source scans
- Seven-pillar NITDA strategic-roadmap presentation

- FCDO/KPMG ministerial scorecard reproduced from the supplied document
- Service-to-people categories for simple/layman understanding
- National honours, academic distinctions and commendations
- International footprint/countries listed in the biography
- Key-source-page gallery
- Additional source scans from biography and introductory chapters

## Evidence principle

All biographical and achievement statements are presented as claims documented by the supplied publication, not as an independent audit. The site continues to distinguish the source record from independent verification.
